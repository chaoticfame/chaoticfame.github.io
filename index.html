04. Deployment Sequence

PORT MAP: Map DHT11 to Pin 7, Buzzer to Pin 8, and Motor to Pin 9[cite: 30, 32, 34]. LDR requires Analog A0[cite: 38].
UPLINK: Establish USB bridge between Arduino Uno and Host Terminal[cite: 39].
COMPILE: Inject firmware via Arduino IDE[cite: 40].
MONITOR: Initialize Serial Stream at 9600 Baud[cite: 41].

05. Integrity & Safety Protocols

  • Avoid aqueous exposure (H2O Hazard)[cite: 61].
  • NMOS isolation required for motor safety[cite: 63].
  • Verify COM port integrity if serial link fails[cite: 67].
